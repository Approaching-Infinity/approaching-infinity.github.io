I was a guest on the Compliance Podcast Network, hosted by Tom Fox. Re-uploading here with credit to Tom and his team so our audience can catch it.

About the host: Tom Fox is a lawyer and the founder of the Compliance Podcast Network, where he has built one of the most established podcast catalogs in corporate compliance. He is known as the Voice of Compliance, and his shows cover governance, ethics, risk, and regulatory practice for the people who actually do the work.

What we covered: Tom and I get into why traditional, static compliance checklists fail to protect complex organizations operating in fast-evolving, ambiguous environments, and how leaders can blend systems engineering, algorithmic auditing, and military leadership principles into risk management processes that drive real operational clarity.

I make the case that standard checklists are insufficient for complex systems, and that effective risk management needs continuously evolving, documented procedures rather than static protocols. Treat internal rules and regulatory compliance as programmatic processes you can monitor continuously, and system breakdowns surface a lot faster.

We also talk about the limits of automation. Algorithmic auditing and AI can dramatically streamline quantitative data checks, but human oversight stays essential anywhere requirements are nuanced or open to interpretation. I share what my military background taught me about building clear structures so people can act decisively in ambiguous environments where complete information is rarely available.

We close on financial literacy as a risk management strategy. Teams across corporate departments comply far better with operational rules when they genuinely understand the purpose behind them.
