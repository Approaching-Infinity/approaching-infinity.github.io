I was a guest on the I AM CEO Podcast, hosted by Gresh W. Harkless Jr. Re-uploading here with credit to Gresh and his team so our audience can catch it.

About the host: Gresh W. Harkless Jr. is an entrepreneur, media strategist, and host of the I AM CEO Podcast, dedicated to highlighting CEOs, founders, and business leaders while sharing insights on growth, leadership, and success.

What we covered: Gresh and I get into how my background in electrical engineering and service in the Air Force provided the foundation for founding Limitless Capital. I walk through how structured discipline applies to navigating complex financial landscapes, and why focusing on risk management and controlling exposure is far more essential than trying to predict unpredictable market outcomes.

We also discuss what true stewardship means for executive leadership, the importance of maintaining alignment and skin in the game, and practical time-management strategies such as blocking off non-negotiable calendar time for family and personal commitments.
