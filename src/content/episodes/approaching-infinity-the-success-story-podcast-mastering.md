I was a guest on the Success Story Podcast, hosted by Scott D. Clary. Re-uploading here with credit to Scott and his team so our audience can catch it.

About the host: Scott D. Clary is an entrepreneur, investor, and host of the Success Story Podcast, where he interviews leaders, executives, and operators to break down the mindsets, strategies, and frameworks behind building impactful businesses.

What we covered: Scott and I get into my transition from working on SpaceX rockets in military space launch operations to building an investment fund at Limitless Capital. I break down how financial engineering mirrors aerospace engineering, approaching both as structured problem-solving challenges rather than emotional decisions.

We dive into the core principles of investing, including why defining your time horizon, targeted returns, and risk tolerance is essential before allocating capital. I share why overthinking and abandoning your thesis is the biggest trap for investors, and how inexperience can actually serve as a strategic advantage when taking calculated risks.

We close on the psychology of entrepreneurship, transitioning away from W-2 security, and maintaining long-term discipline. I share my perspective on why failures are simply lessons learned, why hedge funds are versatile tools rather than inherently high-risk vehicles, and why keeping a strong, adaptable mindset is key to building something lasting.
