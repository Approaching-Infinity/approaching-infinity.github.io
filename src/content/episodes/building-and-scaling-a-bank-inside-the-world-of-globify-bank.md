In this episode of Approaching Infinity, we have Giulio Sartori a partner and project manager at Globify Bank in Zurich, Switzerland. He was also the group CEO at Globify Holding, a privately held investment fund based in New York, which focuses on high-yield real estate and other companies with long-term growth potential.

Prior to his current roles, he worked in trading activities at Fineco Bank and as a co-founder of the medical division of GEA S.r.l. In his various roles, Giulio has gained experience in product development, negotiation, and team coordination.

He has also invented and developed several innovative medical devices and successfully established business collaborations with leading companies in the industry. Giulio also attended Harvard Business School where he focused on finance and accounting for alternative investments as well as strategy execution.
