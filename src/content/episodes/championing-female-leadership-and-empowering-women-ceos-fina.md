In this episode of Approaching Infinity, we're joined by Patricia Lizarraga, the managing partner at Hypatia Capital, known for its steadfast commitment to promoting gender equity in leadership roles across various financial platforms.

Patricia's journey from her early career days to her influential role at Hypatia Capital is an inspiring testament to her passion and determination.

Our conversation navigates through Patricia's pathway, revealing her driving forces, the challenges she has faced, and her tenacity in promoting women-led companies.

We delve into the significant impact of Hypatia Women CEO ETF (WCEO), an innovative concept investing in all American public companies with female CEOs.

We also explore the creation and implications of the Hypatia Women CEO Index and the Hypatia Women Hedge Fund Index, offering insights into their conception, scaling, and the responses they have generated. Patricia's journey offers valuable insights for women aspiring to leadership roles, professionals in the financial sector, and anyone interested in the advancement of gender equity in business.

Whether you're an entrepreneur, a professional aiming to make a difference, or simply someone passionate about gender equity, this episode offers an in-depth look into the efforts being made to champion women in the financial industry.
