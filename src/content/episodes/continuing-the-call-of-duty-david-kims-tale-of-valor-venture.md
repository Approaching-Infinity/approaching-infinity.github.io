In this episode of Approaching Infinity, we are honored to host Mr. David Kim, a decorated military veteran and a formidable presence in the global business arena. David's exemplary life, shaped by his personal motto - God, country, family, displays a transition from distinguished military service to an entrepreneur and philanthropist with a significant imprint in global business ventures.

His journey from a West Point graduate and combat veteran, to becoming a Partner at Apax Partners, demonstrates a transition replete with strategic acumen and relentless perseverance. With 29 fruitful years in the realm of private equity, David’s narrative reveals how a meticulous and strategic approach can usher in broad-spectrum success, transcending geographical and industrial boundaries.

During our engaging conversation, we delve into the fabric of David’s strategic decision-making process, honed over nearly three decades of investment experience, and how these principles can be a beacon for those curious about the world of private equity. We venture into his altruistic quest as the co-founder and CEO of the Children of Fallen Patriots Foundation, illuminating the profound impact of his philanthropic endeavors on the lives of military families. As we delve deeper, David shares precious insights on balancing multiple pivotal roles, an exercise that showcases his adept leadership and unwavering commitment to every sphere of his life.

Whether you are a military veteran navigating the transition to civilian life, an aspiring entrepreneur, or a philanthropy enthusiast, this episode with David Kim is a treasure trove of real-world experiences and sagacious advice. His narrative is not just a testimony to his diverse roles but also a manifestation of a keen sense of duty, be it towards the country, the global business landscape, or the noble cause of supporting the Children of Fallen Patriots.
