This episode welcomes Tom Cobin, the founder of Dynamicoach, for a conversation on communication mastery. Bringing experience from television newsrooms and pharmaceutical boardrooms, Cobin shares his approach to helping leaders elevate their presence.

Cobin emphasizes that effective communication is always about the audience rather than the speaker. He suggests that by focusing outward and aiming for transformation in how stakeholders think, feel, and act, leaders can redefine how they show up.

For founders managing dense investor materials, Cobin advises using a storytelling structure to translate complex data. He outlines the three key elements of any successful presentation as content, structure, and delivery, urging technical leaders to explain the real-world utility and impact of their work.

Cobin integrates neuroscience into his evidence-based workshops, highlighting techniques like deep abdominal breathing to calm nerves and strategic pausing to improve audience retention. Ultimately, he encourages leaders to cultivate authentic presence rather than attempting to clone others.
