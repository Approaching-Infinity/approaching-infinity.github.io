In this episode of Approaching Infinity, guest Eric Radez, President of Strategic Media & Public Relations, shares insights from his multifaceted career across entrepreneurship, financial advising, and investor relations. Radez reflects on his journey from founding an aftermarket auto business to entering financial markets, where he co-chaired the Chicago Resource Expo and helped growth companies connect with targeted investor audiences.

Radez discusses the structural evolution of corporate storytelling and investor engagement, highlighting the transition from traditional in-person expos to dynamic, multi-platform digital strategies. He emphasizes that while direct interaction remains valuable for establishing executive credibility, expanding digital outreach enables micro-cap and natural resource firms to engage broader audiences efficiently.

Examining the specialized dynamics of the mining and natural resource sectors, Radez outlines key variables that determine project success or failure. He explains that evaluating early-stage ventures requires prioritizing hard technical data, such as drill results, while carefully assessing executive management quality and local regulatory jurisdictions.

Finally, Radez provides practical perspective on modern public relations, regulatory compliance, and communications strategy. He underscores the importance of maintaining strict compliance discipline, avoiding speculative promotional hype, and leveraging targeted digital channels to articulate a clear, long-term corporate vision.
