In this episode of Approaching Infinity, we have the privilege to chat with Brian Rathjen, an influential figurehead at Roberts & Ryan Investments, renowned for being a Service Disabled Veteran Owned Broker Dealer.

Brian's remarkable journey from military service to the financial sector is an inspiring tale of resilience and passion. Our discussion follows Brian's path, from his days serving the nation to making strategic business decisions at Roberts & Ryan Investments.

Throughout his journey, the military ethos has been a constant influence, shaping his leadership style and driving him to create opportunities for fellow veterans in the finance industry.

We delve into the unique mission of Roberts & Ryan Investments and its role in leading the charge to highlight the value of Veterans in the financial industry. Brian's story provides rich insights for Veterans transitioning into civilian careers and for anyone navigating significant career shifts.

Whether you're a veteran considering a career in finance, a professional inspired by stories of determination, or an entrepreneur seeking to make an impact, this episode promises a deep dive into the intersection of military service and finance.
