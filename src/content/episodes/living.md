In this episode of Approaching Infinity, host Justin Roopnarine speaks with Dr. Gaby Cora, CEO and founder of CoraLG Inc. and co-founder and Chief Medical Officer at Empathy Encoded. Dr. Cora is a physician, executive coach, and international speaker with decades of experience advancing neuroscience research, pioneering digital health innovation, and helping leaders manage burnout. The conversation explores her career path and her work bridging the gaps between healthcare, human behavior, and artificial intelligence.

Dr. Cora details her professional journey from medical school to conducting clinical research at the National Institutes of Health (NIH) and advising leaders across major health and pharmaceutical organizations. She explains how early work with medical databases shaped her framework for pattern recognition across patient behavior, clinical outcome data, and healthcare systemic gaps. Throughout her career, Dr. Cora emphasizes the importance of building interdisciplinary bridges between technology, data analytics, and clinical medicine to enhance healthcare delivery.

Addressing the widespread challenge of executive stress, Dr. Cora shares insights from her framework, Leading Under Pressure. She outlines how leaders can proactively identify burnout triggers within their teams by aligning personal and corporate values, improving organizational workflows, and prioritizing holistic lifestyle strategies. According to Dr. Cora, transitioning from reactive problem-solving to proactive solution-finding allows individuals and organizations to optimize performance while maintaining long-term wellness.

Finally, the discussion highlights E.M.A (Emotional Management Avatar), a gamified AI application developed by Empathy Encoded to support emotional self-awareness and mental wellness. Dr. Cora describes how the platform enables users to log emotional states, track intensity, and generate chronological reports for care teams, coaches, or personal self-reflection. She emphasizes the necessity of pairing clinical expertise and human empathy with artificial intelligence to expand access to preventative mental health tools safely.
