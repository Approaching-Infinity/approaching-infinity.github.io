In this episode of Approaching Infinity, we delve into the entrepreneurial world with Tim Cooley, a seasoned entrepreneur and trusted adviser to hundreds of burgeoning start-ups.

Having dedicated a decade to the start-up scene, Tim shares invaluable insights on angel investing and venture capital, illuminating the intricacies of the fundraising process.

His wealth of experience is reflected in the success stories of the companies he has guided - firms that now generate millions in annual revenue and have collectively raised over $200 million in venture funding. Join us as Tim demystifies the art of pitching ideas effectively and provides key advice to CEOs looking to navigate the fundraising landscape.

This episode is a treasure trove of knowledge for any start up enthusiast, budding entrepreneur, or business leader aiming to understand the financial dynamics of their industry better
