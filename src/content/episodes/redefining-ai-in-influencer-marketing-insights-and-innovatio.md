In this episode of Approaching Infinity, we are thrilled to host Jeremy Barnett, the innovative CEO of RAD AI, a trailblazer in the realm of data informed influencer marketing and content authenticity.

Known for leveraging the power of artificial intelligence to refine marketing strategies, Jeremy's dynamic approach promises to enlighten entrepreneurs and marketing professionals alike.

Our conversation traces Jeremy's professional journey leading up to the founding of RAD AI, highlighting the pivotal moments that culminated in the company's unique niche within the industry. We further dive into the future of AI in marketing, exploring the potential of technology to revolutionize the sector.

For aspiring tech entrepreneurs, especially those keen on bridging AI and traditional industries in a unique and impactful way, Jeremy imparts invaluable advice. His words are not just for tech enthusiasts but for all seeking to navigate their professional journeys effectively.

Whether you're an AI enthusiast, a marketing professional, or an entrepreneur looking to delve into influencer marketing, this episode promises a wealth of knowledge and inspiration!
