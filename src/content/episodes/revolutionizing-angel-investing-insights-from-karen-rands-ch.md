In this episode of Approaching Infinity, we welcome Karen Rands, President of Kugarand Capital Holdings, a seasoned business consultant, strategic advisor, and passionate advocate of the Compassionate Capitalist Movement. With over two decades of experience working with diverse stakeholders, from entrepreneurs and angel investors to venture capitalists, Karen offers a unique insight into the mechanisms of successful investment and business growth.

She also shares valuable perspectives from her Compassionate Capitalist Radio Podcast, a renowned platform that amplifies the importance of wealth creation through entrepreneurial success. As a best-selling author of "Inside Secrets to Angel Investing," Karen enlightens our audience on the intricacies of effective angel investing, making this episode a must-listen for investors and entrepreneurs aiming to navigate the complex landscape of capital growth and investment!
