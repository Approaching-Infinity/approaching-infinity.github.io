In this episode of Approaching Infinity, host Justin Roopnarine sits down with Andrew Devon Stephenson, Don Randall, and Evan Mills from Zera TechBridge to examine the widening technical talent gap across the Department of Defense and federal government agencies. Stephenson explains that the rapid acceleration of technology, particularly in artificial intelligence and cloud computing, has outpaced traditional workforce pipeline capacities. This gap leaves critical national security roles unfilled across uniform, civilian, and contractor sectors.

The guests explore how the traditional security clearance process creates substantial delays for young talent and transitioning service members seeking cleared tech positions. Randall highlights how lengthy processing times prevent qualified individuals from entering the workforce quickly, often causing prolonged gaps between hiring and active employment. To address this challenge, Stephenson outlines how Zera TechBridge streamlines school sponsorships and connects candidates directly to reserve components, enabling earlier clearance processing and faster onboarding.

Mills and Stephenson discuss how gamified platforms and practical skill building offer effective alternatives to traditional higher education for emerging technical roles. Mills emphasizes that self-taught developers and high school graduates can acquire high-level software and cloud engineering skills through modern digital tools. Stephenson details Zera TechBridge's initiative to rank and vet technical certifications, creating a transparent due-diligence framework that helps students select valuable upskilling pathways and allows hiring authorities to evaluate candidate qualifications accurately.

Looking toward the future of national security, the guests stress the growing importance of human-in-the-loop workflows, autonomous systems, and modern cyber capabilities. Randall and Stephenson emphasize the vital role of veteran mentorship, noting that experienced military leaders can help guide the next generation into high-impact technologist roles. By creating an end-to-end talent portal, Zera TechBridge aims to empower students and veterans alike to serve their country through advanced technical expertise.
