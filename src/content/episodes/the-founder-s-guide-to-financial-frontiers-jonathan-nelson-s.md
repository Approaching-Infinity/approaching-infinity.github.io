In this episode of Approaching Infinity, we're thrilled to welcome Jonathan Nelson, a vanguard in the venture capital world and a pivotal figure in global startup ecosystems. Jonathan shares his expert insights on the complexities smaller companies encounter when attempting to list on U.S. stock markets, underscoring the necessity for robust capital markets in developing regions such as South America and Africa. His deep dive into the historical and current dynamics of capital markets reveals the challenges and opportunities that lie within.

Jonathan's extensive career journey, from founding the largest global network of tech founders to his influential roles on the SEC Advisory Committee and the Board of the Wilson Center for Public Policy in Latin America, provides a rich backdrop for our discussion. He elaborates on his mission to grow economies and alleviate poverty by facilitating better conditions for tech founders worldwide, drawing on lessons learned from his time across various global regions.

Listeners will gain a better understanding of the hurdles smaller companies face in the capital markets, the critical role of international development in fostering accessible capital markets, and the broader implications for economic growth and innovation. Jonathan's personal anecdotes and professional experiences offer valuable lessons on perseverance, strategic thinking, and the importance of creating a more inclusive and equitable global financial landscape.

Join us in this episode as Jonathan Nelson charts a course toward a future where the capital markets are more accessible and equitable, providing a beacon of hope and a roadmap for tech founders and investors alike!
