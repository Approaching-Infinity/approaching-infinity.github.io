In this episode of Approaching Infinity, host Justin Roopnarine is joined by Warren Ross, Partner at JLC Income Fund and deal maker at Jets Capital. Ross explains the hidden market of uncollected civil court judgments, where an estimated 80 percent of awarded judgments in the United States go unpaid. He points out that while winning a lawsuit produces a court-approved debt, the legal system does not automatically enforce payment, leaving many creditors without recovery.

Ross details how combining financial investigation with legal enforcement bridges a critical gap in debt recovery. He highlights how forensic analysis and fraud examination can uncover hidden bank accounts, transferred real estate, and assets concealed by strategic non-payers. According to Ross, applying targeted legal pressure once assets are identified transforms uncollectible judgments into actionable opportunities, forcing non-compliant debtors to fulfill their court obligations.

Ross outlines the operational strategy of Acorn Funding Group and JLC Income Fund, which purchase uncollected judgments at a discount to provide immediate liquidity to creditors. He notes that pooling these judgments allows passive investors to participate in funding enforcement and litigation. Ross stresses that rigorous pre-vetting and asset location analysis are essential to ensuring high success rates when attempting to recover funds.

Beyond judgment enforcement, Ross discusses his work with Jets Capital, facilitating high-value networking for family offices and investors in unique venues such as private jet hangars. He emphasizes that long-term success across sales and capital raising stems from prioritizing relationships, maintaining strong ethics, and delivering genuine value. Ross shares his perspective that focusing on meaningful connections remains the single most impactful factor in building a lasting business legacy.
