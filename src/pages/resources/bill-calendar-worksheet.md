# Bill calendar worksheet

**Free · Updated October 30, 2026**

Use this original Approaching Infinity worksheet privately. Start with three bills; add more if useful. Use short labels and leave out account numbers, passwords and identifying details. You do not need to send this page to anyone.

Keep the due date separate from the date you plan to send payment or expect an automatic withdrawal. Check payment timing with the biller and payment service. Mark uncertain amounts “estimate” and unknown details “check.” A calendar entry does not make a payment.

## 2. Put the planned dates on the calendar

Number the boxes for your chosen month. Leave boxes outside that month blank. In the appropriate box, add the short bill label and amount on its planned payment/withdrawal date. If useful, add expected income dates and mark them “expected.” Treat uncertain income separately from money already available. This calendar supports months that occupy up to six calendar rows.
